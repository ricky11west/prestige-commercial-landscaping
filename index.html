About Our Company

Built on Handshakes. Sustained by Results.

When Bill Hargrove started Prestige Commercial Landscaping in 1989 with a single truck and a two-man crew, his philosophy was simple: show up on time, do the job right, and stand behind your work.

Thirty-five years later, that philosophy hasn't changed - even as we've grown to serve over 200 commercial properties across the Greater Austin area. We still treat every property like our reputation depends on it. Because it does.

Bill Hargrove
Founder & President
What We Do

Full-Service Commercial Landscaping

From routine maintenance to complete landscape transformations, we deliver the full spectrum of services commercial properties require.

Grounds Maintenance

Weekly mowing, edging, blowing, and detailed property upkeep. We keep your grounds pristine year-round, on a schedule you can set your watch to.

Landscape Design & Installation

Custom design, planting, and hardscape installation that enhances property value and curb appeal. Drought-tolerant options built for the Texas climate.

Irrigation Systems

Design, installation, repair, and seasonal management of commercial irrigation. Smart controllers and water-efficient solutions that save money.

Tree & Shrub Care

Pruning, trimming, fertilization, and disease management by ISA-certified arborists who understand Central Texas species inside and out.

Seasonal Color Programs

Rotating flower beds and seasonal plantings that keep your property looking fresh and inviting every month of the year.

Landscape Lighting

Architectural and security lighting that showcases your property after hours and enhances safety for tenants and visitors alike.
